Frequently
asked questions

How long can a battery power a home?

It depends on the sizing and what you power.
In practice, a well-sized residential system can
provide 6 to 72 hours, and even more if you only keep the essentials.

Is it automatic during a power outage?

Yes. When an outage occurs, the system automatically detects
the interruption and powers your home's essential circuits
without manual intervention.

Is it cost-effective in Pakistan?

Yes, absolutely. By combining solar panels, batteries
and IESCO's pricing programs, you can significantly
reduce your dependence on the grid and save several
hundred dollars per year.
